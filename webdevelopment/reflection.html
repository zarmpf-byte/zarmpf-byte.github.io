Experience & Goals

My professional background combines hands-on technical experience with formal education in Information Technology. I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at the University of Missouri-Columbia, where I am building a strong foundation in computer systems, networking, and modern IT practices. Prior to this, I earned an Associate of Science in Electronics Engineering Technology from State Technical College of Missouri, which provided me with extensive experience working with electronic systems and troubleshooting complex technical problems. My education complements the practical technical skills I have developed throughout my career.

In my current role as a Maintenance Electrician, I work extensively with industrial control systems, PLCs, robotics, and computer-based equipment. My responsibilities include troubleshooting AC/DC electrical circuits, installing production equipment, modifying machine language programs, and setting up computer systems with various software applications. This position has strengthened my problem-solving abilities while giving me experience working with technology in a real-world environment. The role has also helped me develop strong organizational, communication, and strategic thinking skills while supporting multi-million dollar industrial systems.

Earlier in my career, I worked as an Electronics Technician installing and maintaining HVAC control systems across multiple businesses ranging from government facilities to private enterprises. In this role, I diagnosed and repaired low-voltage control systems while ensuring communication between on-site systems and remote monitoring systems. I also served in the United States Marine Corps as an Artillery Sensor Support Marine, where I operated advanced sensor equipment, GPS systems, and digital communication systems to support artillery operations in high-stress environments. These experiences taught me the importance of precision, teamwork, and reliability when working with critical technology systems and a mission to complete.

Looking ahead, my goal is to build a career in Information Technology where I can apply my technical background and continue expanding my skills. I am particularly interested in contributing to IT and operational technology support environments, where both computer systems and industrial technologies intersect. Ultimately, I hope to leverage my combined experience in electronics, industrial systems, and IT to help organizations improve system reliability, efficiency, and technological capabilities.
